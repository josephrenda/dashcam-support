Quick Troubleshooting

  • Enable Camera, Microphone, and Location permissions in iOS Settings.
  • Keep enough free storage for saved clips and temporary recordings.
  • If a clip is missing, reopen the app and check the clip counter on the camera view.
  • For YouTube sharing, upload as Unlisted and paste the URL in the incident report.
