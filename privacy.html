Privacy Policy

For DashCam Guardian mobile application

Effective date: 2026-08-07

1. Information We Collect

  • Account and contact details you choose to provide when contacting support.
  • App-generated data such as settings choices, incident report details, and optional YouTube URLs you submit.
  • Device and diagnostic information needed to operate and troubleshoot the app.
  • Location data when you grant permission, used for map and route context.
  • Camera and microphone data when recording is active, including saved clips you choose to keep or attach.

2. How We Use Information

  • Provide core app features, including recording, clip saving, and incident reporting.
  • Show route and location context for navigation and incident records.
  • Improve performance, reliability, and user support.
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce app terms.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We may share data with service providers that help operate app features (for example, hosting, diagnostics, or mapping providers), or when required by law.

4. YouTube Links and Third-Party Services

If you include a YouTube URL in an incident report, that content is governed by your YouTube privacy settings and Google policies. We recommend using Unlisted visibility when sharing sensitive footage.

5. Data Retention

We retain data only as long as needed for app functionality, support, legal compliance, and legitimate business purposes. You can remove local app data by uninstalling the app or clearing data where available.

6. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards to protect information. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

7. Your Choices

  • You can manage permissions (camera, microphone, location) in iOS Settings at any time.
  • You can choose whether to submit incident details and external links.
  • You can contact us to request data-related assistance where applicable by law.

8. Children's Privacy

DashCam Guardian is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post updates on this page and revise the effective date.

10. Contact Us

For privacy questions, contact: support@dashcamguardian.app
